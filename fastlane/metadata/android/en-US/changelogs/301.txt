### New Features

- Multi-part videos (BiliBili P1, P2, ...): playing one part now keeps going with the other parts, and the new "All parts" action in search results and the song menu lets you play, download or add every part to a playlist at once
- Playlist and album links (YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud sets, Bandcamp albums, NicoNico and PeerTube playlists) now open as a song list with play all, download all and add to playlist; channel links show a clear message instead of an error

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed the previous song continuing to play while the next song was still loading
