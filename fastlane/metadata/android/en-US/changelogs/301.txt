### New Features

- Multi-part videos (BiliBili P1, P2, ...): playing one part now keeps going with the other parts, and the new "All parts" action in search results and the song menu lists every part
- The parts list lets you pick parts one by one or select all, then play, download, delete downloads, add to a playlist, or merge them in order into one song in your library
- Playlist and album links (YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud sets, Bandcamp albums, NicoNico and PeerTube playlists) now open as a song list with the same batch actions; channel links show a clear message instead of an error
- Playlists can now be searched and sorted by title, artist, album, duration, recently played or most played, in either direction

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed the previous song continuing to play while the next song was still loading
- The "more" button in the play queue now opens a menu (play, play next, move up or down, remove, more options) instead of removing the song right away
