PipeMusic is a music-first, libre Android player built on the PipePipe codebase.

It keeps PipePipe's multi-platform extraction and download engine, but replaces the video-oriented interface with a focused listening experience: search for music across supported services, stream audio, download tracks for offline listening, import your own library from a folder, build playlists, and enjoy a modern Compose UI with multiple themes and a customizable accent color.

PipeMusic is an independent fork. It is derived from PipePipe, which in turn is a fork of NewPipe. PipeMusic is not affiliated with or endorsed by the PipePipe or NewPipe projects.

Features:
- Search across YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, BiliBili, Niconico, PeerTube, media.ccc.de and other services supported by the bundled extractor.
- Stream audio, queue tracks, and use sequential, repeat-all, repeat-one and shuffle playback.
- Download audio into the app's private library when the source permits direct audio download.
- Import an entire folder of local audio into the private library, with metadata and embedded artwork.
- Create playlists, favorite tracks, and browse recently played or recently added music.
- Background playback with a media notification, lock-screen controls, headset controls and a sleep timer.
- Eight preset themes, a custom accent color picker, light/dark/system/AMOLED modes, Android 12+ dynamic color and artwork-based tinting.
